# How to mount bellows  
This, unfortunately, is one of the weakest parts of this build. These parts are quite important to keep dust away from your sensitive lead screws and linear guides.  
In the future, I plan to mount all of them with M3 screws but as of right now you need to hot glue some of the bellows.

# Printing the bellows
You need to print these flat on the bed with 0.12mm layer height, you should get only two layers. After printing you need to bend it back and forth.

# Y bellow
Print two bellows, you will have one at each side of the carriage.
Thread the fishing line (or other wire) through the holes of the bellows and pass it through the hole in the rod holder, do this for both sides then clamp both the wires and the bellow with a screw fixed to the heatset insert of the rod holder, use two pieces of wire, cut them long and refine it later.  
To fix the other end to the carriage hotglue them, you can also cut it a bit with scissors if convinient.  
You should try to get them so that the bellow when folding tends to go down a bit.   
(You can also always glue the rod holder part too of course)
